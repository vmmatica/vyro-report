Seedance 2.0 · normal / fast / mini3/10Looks good, but is not yet very viable for final product placement. It supports up to eight reference photos plus a start frame, which is unusually useful for testing a product for the first time.Previewing how a new product may look before committing to a production workflow.
Kling 3.07/10Very strong visual quality, product consistency, and realistic physics. It accepts only a start frame and an end frame, so complicated products may be harder to control.Simple products where appearance and physical interaction need to stay stable.
Wan 2.77/10Comparable overall to Kling, with a tendency to interact with the product more. Physics are a clear strength, but it does not support multiple reference-image uploads.Physics-led concepts and product interaction with a single reference setup.
Gemini Flash1/10The output looks noticeably synthetic. Its low cost can still make it useful for early, broad-audience concept tests where photorealism is not the priority.Budget experimentation and quick first-pass ideas.
Marketing Studio + UGC10/10Very high potential for repeatable product placement: upload a product website and/or many product photos, then reuse the product across multiple formats. Full testing is still pending because videos consume credits despite the 24-hour unlimited window.Best current direction for scalable, consistent product content.

Capability notes

Reference control: Seedance supports multiple reference photos plus a start frame. Kling 3.0 and Wan 2.7 are limited to a single-reference setup in this comparison.

Product ingestion: Marketing Studio + UGC can ingest an entire product from a website/product link and reuse it across different formats.
