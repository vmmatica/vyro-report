Kling 3.0 product placement

A visual, repeatable workflow for turning one prepared product image into a short video while keeping object physics believable.

Step 1

Open Cinema Studio

Select Cinema Studio from the top navigation.

Step 2

Select Kling 3.0

Choose Video → Kling 3.0.

Choose your model
Step 3

Add one strong start frame

Drop your prepared product image into START FRAME.

1 · Start frame
Skip the end frame unless you need a specific final pose. One strong start frame usually gives the model enough direction.
Step 4

Describe movement and physics

Describe the movement and protect the physics.

Movement prompt
Slow, natural camera movement around the product. Respect gravity, contact, weight, and realistic momentum. Preserve shape, materials, and placement. No melting, warping, floating, or invented parts.
Step 5

Set resolution and length

Pick a resolution and length. 720p · 8s is a clean starting point.
