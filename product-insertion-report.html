Product insertion starting frames

A focused review of 27 ChatGPT-made product-insertion images. Every example listed in the campaign reference was completed.

Open campaign reference ↗

Methodology

All 27 product-insertion examples were made with ChatGPT. This review looks at whether each image is strong enough to work as a starting frame for video.

What worked

Clear space

Open, uncluttered areas give the product room to sit naturally in the composition.

Lighting contrast

Clear separation between light and shadow helps the product read with stronger shape and material.

Natural starting point

The frame still needs to feel organic before it becomes a useful input for video generation.
