Marketing Studio product UGC

A simple workflow for turning a product link or image set into traditional creator-led product content across formats.

Step 1

Open the product workspace

Open this URL
higgsfield.ai/marketing-studio/product
Step 2

Create the product

Use a link or upload images. Then select the product tile.

Best practice: use a product website when available, or upload a clean set of product images with consistent lighting and angles.
Step 3

Choose the format

Start with UGC for creator-led product content.

Step 4

Write the prompt

Describe the action, benefit, and tone. Let the preset handle the social-video structure.

UGC prompt pattern
Create a natural creator-led product video showing the product in use. Keep the product appearance accurate and unchanged. Highlight one clear benefit, use conversational language, and make the action feel native to short-form social video.
Step 5

Click Generate

Check the product, format, and prompt. Then click GENERATE.

Important limitation: Marketing Studio can look excellent, but it is not the best tool for replicating a custom starting frame or a reference example outside its presets.
