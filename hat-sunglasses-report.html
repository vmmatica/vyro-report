Hat + sunglasses

A focused comparison of three Higgsfield workflows on the new Hat + sunglasses product. This report is shareable without the research-site password.

Current readout

Kling 3.0 respects the object physics well. It is the strongest result in this round for keeping the hat and sunglasses behaving like a coherent physical object.

Seedance 2.0 still looks fake. The result is useful as a quick visual test, but it does not yet read as convincing product footage for this item.

Marketing Studio is good for traditional product UGC, with an important limitation here. It produces polished output and is well suited to preset-led UGC formats, but it cannot reliably replicate from a supplied starting frame or starting example when that reference is outside its preset workflow.
